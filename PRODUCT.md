# SageLift product

## Purpose

SageLift is a simple, personal fitness and daily-progress app. It supports steady progress through consistent training, health habits, and clear daily direction.

## Core experience

The main experience is a morning **Today** screen that immediately answers:

> What do I need to do today?

Today will eventually show:

- Today's workout
- Protein
- Water
- Weight and BMI check-in
- Habits
- Challenge progress

Workout logging must be fast and practical to use with one hand.

## Product principles

- Keep the interface calm, modern, dark-first, and touch-friendly.
- Work offline without paid external services.
- Make daily actions clear and quick rather than information-dense.
- Avoid turning SageLift into a complicated bodybuilding spreadsheet.

## Future direction

SageOS and voice integration may be added later, after the core personal fitness experience is reliable and useful on its own.
